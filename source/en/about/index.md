I’m **Sibo Wang**, an engineer at [Alibaba](https://www.alibabagroup.com/en-US), developing financial systems and AI agents for Taobao and Tmall. My previous work spans [international commerce](/en/2026/01/04/cross-border-ecommerce-transaction-flow/) and [transaction fund management](/en/2025/09/17/what-is-digtal-rmb/). I’m looking to focus more deeply on building AI agents for production and applying them to business needs.

I received an MSc in Information Technology from the [Hong Kong University of Science and Technology](https://hkust.edu.hk/) in 2025 (full-time, 2024–2025), following a bachelor’s degree in Software Engineering from [Zhengzhou University](https://english.zzu.edu.cn/) in 2021 (2017–2021).

I joined Alibaba in July 2021 after completing my undergraduate degree and have been there ever since. I spent three years ensuring the reliability of international commerce transactions and working on fund settlement, before shifting my focus to financial systems and AI agents. Before joining full-time, I interned at [Zhongyuan Bank](https://www.zybank.com.cn/zybank/investor_relations81/corporate_information40/index.html) from September 2020 to March 2021, and at Alibaba from May to August 2020.

Outside work, I enjoy running, reading, and travel. I share engineering practice and reflections on life through this blog.
