I’m Sibo Wang, an engineer at Alibaba, working on financial systems and AI agents. My experience spans international business, AI agents, and finance. Going forward, I plan to focus on AI agent engineering and its applications in business.

I received an MSc in Information Technology from the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology in 2025 (full-time, 2024–2025), following a bachelor’s degree in Software Engineering from Zhengzhou University in 2021 (2017–2021).

I joined Alibaba in July 2021 and have worked there since, with a current focus on financial systems and AI agents. Before that, I interned at Zhongyuan Bank from September 2020 to March 2021, and at Alibaba from May to August 2020.

Outside work, I enjoy running, reading, films, and travel. I share engineering practice and reflections on life through this blog.

You can reach me by email, on GitHub, or on WeChat at allinwxx. You can also follow my WeChat Official Account, 王星星的魔灯, and find me on Xiaohongshu (RedNote).
