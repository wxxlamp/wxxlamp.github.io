I’m **Sibo Wang**, an engineer at [Alibaba](https://www.alibabagroup.com/en-US), working on financial systems and AI agents. My experience spans [international business](/en/2026/01/04/cross-border-ecommerce-transaction-flow/), [AI agents](/en/2026/07/19/agentscope-ai-operations-platform-v1/), and [finance](/en/2025/09/17/what-is-digtal-rmb/). Going forward, I plan to focus on AI agent engineering and its applications in business.

I received an MSc in Information Technology from the [Hong Kong University of Science and Technology](https://hkust.edu.hk/) in 2025 (full-time, 2024–2025), following a bachelor’s degree in Software Engineering from [Zhengzhou University](https://english.zzu.edu.cn/) in 2021 (2017–2021).

I joined Alibaba in July 2021 and have worked there since, with a current focus on financial systems and AI agents. Before that, I interned at [Zhongyuan Bank](https://www.zybank.com.cn/zybank/investor_relations81/corporate_information40/index.html) from September 2020 to March 2021, and at Alibaba from May to August 2020.

Outside work, I enjoy running, reading, films, and travel. I share engineering practice and reflections on life through this blog.
